Sort the Shipment: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Sort the Shipment ("the game") is a puzzle game by ImadC9. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Summary

The game itself has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect or sell your personal information. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads: optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward (an extra parking spot, a hint or bonus coins), and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To deliver these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in your Android settings),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions and diagnostics (which ads were shown or tapped, crashes and performance of the ad SDK).

This data is sent to Google and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form the first time it starts, so you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change that choice at any time with Settings > Privacy options in the game. Elsewhere, you can opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Google > Ads), and if you live in a US state with a privacy law you can use the same Privacy options button where it is shown. Ads are never required: every reward-for-ad feature can be skipped, and the game is fully playable without watching any.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels unlocked, stars, coins, harbour projects) and settings (music, sound, vibration, reduced motion) are saved in a small file on your device so you can continue where you left off. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for short haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings), internet and network state to load ads, and the advertising ID permission used by Google's ad SDK.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the game's itch.io page. Comments on that page may be public; do not include personal or sensitive information.
